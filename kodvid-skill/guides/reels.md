# Reels guide: women aged 18 to 55

The user wants to grow a following of women aged 18 to 55 on Instagram, fast. Every reel serves that goal:
people stop, watch to the end, **save**, **share** with a friend, and **follow** for more.

## What works for this audience

- **Useful and relatable beats generic.** Practical tips she can use today, small habits, "I thought it was just me" moments.
- **Saves and shares drive reach.** Lists, checklists, tips worth keeping, lines worth sending to a friend.
- **Warm, respectful tone.** Speak like a smart friend: "sen", short sentences, no lecturing, no body shaming, no fear tactics.
- **Topics that tend to work:** morning and evening routines, self-care, skin and hair care basics, home organization, easy recipes,
  motherhood tips, relationships and communication, confidence, money basics for women, wellness habits, motivation.
- **Keep one niche.** A channel that always gives the same kind of value grows faster than one that jumps between topics.
  If the user has not chosen a niche, use the one they mention and say which niche the reel serves.

## Length and pacing

- 15 to 30 s. Shorter is better if the value fits.
- **The first 1.5 s decide everything:** start with a `hook` scene, never with a logo or a slow title.
- A new scene every 2 to 4 s. About 2.5 words of narration per second.
- Captions always on: most people watch without sound.

## Hook formulas (write in Turkish, adapt)

- A question to her: "Sabahların daha güzel geçsin mi?"
- A surprising promise: "3 dakikada daha düzenli bir mutfak"
- A mistake: "Bunu yapıyorsan cildin yorulur"
- A list promise: "Kaydet: stresli günler için 3 küçük şey"
- Relatable: "Herkes uyurken senin aklın da durmuyor mu?"

No clickbait that the reel does not pay off. Highlight the one key word with `highlight`.

## Formats (pick one, then fill the kit)

| Format | Scenes |
|---|---|
| **3 tips** | `hook`, `tip` ×3, `quote` or `statement`, `cta` |
| **Checklist** | `hook`, `list` (4 to 5 items with icons), `statement`, `cta` |
| **Myth vs fact** | `hook`, `statement` (the myth), `statement` (the truth, highlighted), `fact` only with a real source, `cta` |
| **Quote of the day** | `image` (a calm generated image), `quote`, `cta` |
| **Mini story** | `hook`, `statement` ×3 (problem, turn, result), `quote`, `cta` |
| **Before / after habit** | `hook`, `statement` (before), `statement` (after), `list` (how), `cta` |

## Style choice

- `soft`: self-care, beauty, motherhood, relationships, quotes. The default for this audience.
- `fresh`: health, food, home.
- `bold`: motivation, money, trends.

## Call to action

End with a `cta` under 2.5 s. Ask for one main action that fits the reel:
- Tips and checklists: "Kaydet" (save), so she comes back.
- Relatable or quote reels: "Bir arkadaşına gönder" (share).
- Series: "Devamı için takip et" (follow).

## Posting advice for the user (put it in your final report, briefly)

- Add a trending sound from Instagram's library inside the app, at low volume under the voice. It helps reach and avoids copyright claims.
- Write a caption with the main tip repeated, and 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.
- Post consistently: one reel a day is a good target. Batch-produce a week at once.
- Mark AI-generated content when Instagram asks.

## Never

- Invented statistics, studies, doctors or experts. Health, beauty and money tips must be general and safe; no medical or financial promises.
- Real people's faces or celebrity names to borrow attention.
- Shaming, fear or "you are not enough" angles.
